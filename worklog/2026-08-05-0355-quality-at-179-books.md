## Two numbers of mine that overstated the problem

I published heading noise as **19.6%** at 78 books, then **15.0%** at 179. Both
used a **length** rule — which counts `PREFACE` (670 passages) and
`Book  V.` (536) as noise.

Those are real headings. A bare roman numeral carries no information; *"Book V."*
carries the book number. Measured by **shape** instead:

```
structural   6.2%      genuine noise  7.7%      substantive  86.1%
```

The true problem was 7.7%, not 19.6%. **A metric that flags good data as bad
does not just overstate — it invites a fix that destroys structure.**
