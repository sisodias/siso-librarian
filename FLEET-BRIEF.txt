4. A REAL FINDING YOU SHOULD ACT ON — MiniMax caching is OFF

Measured today across 42,431 MiniMax requests: cached_read_tokens = ZERO. Every
~70,000-token call is billed at full rate.

Anthropic on the same gateway caches at ~100% — 10.3 BILLION cached reads, so its
611,000-token calls cost roughly a tenth of face value.

MiniMax DOES support caching. Verified directly against api.minimax.io with an
anthropic-style cache_control block:
  call 1: cache_creation_input_tokens=37, cache_read=0
  call 2: cache_creation=0, cache_read=37   <- it read the cache

So the capability exists and nothing in our stack is using it. Neither
~/.config/go-llm-proxy/minimax-codex.yaml nor the Bifrost Minimax provider config
sets anything cache-related.

Investigate whether the go-llm-proxy translation layer strips cache_control, or
whether the harnesses never send it. If this can be switched on it is the single
biggest efficiency win available — potentially 10x on every MiniMax call. Measure
before and after with cached_read_tokens from the log.
